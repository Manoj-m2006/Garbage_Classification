Frequently Asked Questions

How does the EcoSort CNN AI classify items? +
EcoSort utilizes a MobileNetV2 deep Convolutional Neural Network pre-trained on ImageNet. The AI evaluates edge patterns, textures, reflectivity, and material shape features to categorize items into Cardboard, Glass, Metal, Paper, Plastic, or Trash.
Why is proper garbage classification important? +
Contamination occurs when non-recyclable materials are placed in recycling bins, which can ruin entire batches of recyclable materials. Proper sorting ensures clean recycling streams and reduces landfill overflow.
Can I use the live camera on mobile devices? +
Yes! Click the Live Camera tab and grant camera access. On smartphones, it will default to your rear camera for quick point-and-scan waste sorting.
